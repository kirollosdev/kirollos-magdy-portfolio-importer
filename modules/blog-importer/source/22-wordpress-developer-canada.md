## English and French Content

Canada is officially bilingual, and for businesses that serve Quebec, French is not optional. Quebec's language rules require businesses operating there to offer their website in French, with the French version at least as complete as other languages. With WPML or TranslatePress, the site gets full English and French versions with separate URLs, translated menus, forms, checkout, and SEO fields.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. Confirm your obligations with a Canadian legal professional.

## Privacy and Cookie Consent

Canadian privacy law covers how websites collect personal information, and Quebec's Law 25 adds stricter requirements for consent to non-essential cookies and tracking. In practice, that means:

- A consent banner with clear accept and reject options.
- Analytics and ad pixels that load only after consent.
- An up-to-date privacy policy.
- Email marketing that follows Canada's anti-spam rules, with clear opt-in.

I set up consent tools and configure tracking so it respects the visitor's choice.

## Local SEO for Canadian Cities

Canadian customers search with their city: "same day delivery Mississauga" or "bakery Calgary". A Canadian site should have:

- City or service area pages with real local content.
- Consistent name, address, and phone number on the site and Google Business Profile.
- Local business schema.
- Reviews from Canadian customers.
