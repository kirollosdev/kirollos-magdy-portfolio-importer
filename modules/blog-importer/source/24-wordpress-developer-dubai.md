A WordPress developer for Dubai needs to understand how UAE customers browse and buy: mostly on mobile, often in both Arabic and English, and very often through WhatsApp. I have built websites and online stores for businesses in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, and across the Gulf, and I am a native Arabic speaker, which makes Arabic and right-to-left work natural rather than a translation exercise.

## Arabic and English, Done Properly

Most UAE businesses need both languages. A proper bilingual site has:

- Full Arabic and English versions with separate URLs for SEO.
- A mirrored right-to-left layout for Arabic, including icons, sliders, and forms.
- Arabic fonts that match the brand's English typography.
- Arabic copy written for Arabic readers, not just machine translated.

I cover the details in [building a bilingual Arabic and English WordPress website](/bilingual-wordpress-website-arabic-rtl/).

## Payment Options UAE Customers Use

UAE shoppers expect a range of options: Visa and Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay, PayPal, buy now pay later services like Tabby and Tamara, and cash on delivery in some sectors. I integrate them through global gateways like Stripe and PayPal and regional gateways that support local methods. See [WooCommerce payment gateway integration](/woocommerce-payment-gateway-integration/).

## WhatsApp-First Lead Capture

In the Gulf, many customers prefer to message rather than fill in forms. I add:

- WhatsApp buttons with pre-filled messages that include the product or service.
- Click-to-call on mobile.
- Short forms for customers who prefer them, sent to email, a CRM, or WhatsApp.
