A delivery company website should do more than list services. Customers want a price, a booking, and confidence their package will arrive. Businesses want accounts, invoices, and integrations. The website should handle all of that without phone calls.

I have built websites for delivery companies in Canada, including account-based service displays and system integrations. Here are the features that matter.

Instant Quote Calculator

The first question every visitor has is "How much?" A quote calculator that takes pickup and drop-off locations, package size or weight, and delivery speed, then shows a price immediately, converts far better than "Contact us for a quote". Pricing can be based on distance, zones, or fixed rates.

Online Booking and Payment

After the quote, the customer should book right away: pickup address, recipient details, pickup time, and payment by card or on account. Confirmations go by email or SMS to the customer and the dispatch team.

Shipment Tracking

A tracking page where customers enter a tracking number and see the status reduces support calls a lot. It can connect to your dispatch system or delivery software through an API, or be updated from the WordPress dashboard for smaller operations.

Customer Accounts for Businesses

Business customers send deliveries regularly. Accounts let them:

  • Save addresses and recipients.
  • See delivery history.
  • Download invoices.
  • Access contract pricing.

Showing different services or prices based on account type is something I have built for delivery clients, and it is a strong reason businesses choose one courier over another.

Integrations

Delivery companies usually already use software for dispatch, driver apps, or accounting. With the WordPress REST API and custom plugins, the website can send bookings directly to those systems and pull status updates back, so nobody retypes orders. Online payments work the same way as in a store; see WooCommerce payment gateway integration.

Service and Area Pages

Create a page for each service, such as same-day delivery, courier, freight, or moving, and for each city or area you cover. This helps customers and is the backbone of local SEO for searches like "same day delivery Toronto".

Trust Signals

  • Insurance and coverage details.
  • Business customers and testimonials.
  • Clear terms, delivery times, and prohibited items.
  • Easy contact options, including WhatsApp and phone.

Transportation Services Websites

Passenger transportation, such as airport transfers, chauffeur services, or tour transport, follows the same pattern with a different booking form: pickup and drop-off, date and time, passengers, luggage, and vehicle type, with price calculated by distance or route.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the website calculate prices by distance?

Yes, using a maps API to measure the route and apply your pricing rules.

Can bookings go straight to our dispatch software?

In most cases, yes, through the software's API or webhooks.

Can business customers pay monthly instead of per order?

Yes. Account customers can be set to pay on invoice while others pay by card.
