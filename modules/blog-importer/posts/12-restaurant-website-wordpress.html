A restaurant website in WordPress has one job: turn a hungry visitor into an order or a reservation within a minute. Most people arrive on a phone, often while deciding where to eat right now. If the menu is a slow PDF or the phone number is hard to find, they pick the next place on Google Maps.

These are the features that matter for restaurants, cafes, coffee shops, and food brands.

1. A Mobile Menu That Loads Instantly

Skip the PDF menu. Build the menu as real web content:

  • Loads fast on mobile data.
  • Search engines can read it, so dishes can appear in search.
  • Easy to update prices and items from the dashboard.

I build menus with a custom post type and ACF fields for name, description, price, photo, and dietary tags. The owner edits a simple form, and the menu design updates everywhere.

2. Clear Calls to Action on Every Screen

The top of the page on mobile should show, without scrolling:

  • Order online or Book a table.
  • Call with a tap-to-call link.
  • Directions linking to Google Maps.
  • Opening hours.

3. Online Ordering Without Heavy Commissions

Delivery apps charge large commissions. A WooCommerce-based ordering system on your own site lets regulars order directly: menu items as products, add-ons like extra cheese as options, pickup or delivery, time slots, and online payment or cash on delivery. Orders can arrive by email, on a tablet, or be printed in the kitchen.

4. Table Reservations

A booking form or reservation plugin lets guests choose date, time, and party size, with automatic confirmation emails. Many restaurants start with a simple form and move to a full system when volume grows.

5. Photos That Sell, Without Slowing the Site

Food photography sells, but large photos slow the site. Images should be resized, compressed, served in modern formats, and lazy loaded below the first screen.

6. Local SEO

Most restaurant searches are local, like "coffee shop near me" or "best shawarma in Mississauga". To show up:

  • Put name, address, and phone consistently on the site and in your Google Business Profile.
  • Add restaurant schema markup.
  • Create a page for each branch with its own address, hours, and map.
  • Encourage Google reviews.

7. Multilingual Menus

In cities with international visitors, such as Dubai, a menu in English and Arabic reaches more customers. With WPML, each dish is translated once and stays in sync.

Coffee Shop Websites

A coffee shop website needs the same basics with a different emphasis: atmosphere and brand, a clear menu, locations, and often an online shop for beans, gift cards, or subscriptions through WooCommerce. A loyalty or subscription offer turns one-time visitors into regulars.

Food Brands and Grocery Stores

Packaged food brands and specialty grocers selling online need a full ecommerce store: product filters, delivery zones, minimum order rules, and sometimes stock sync with a point of sale. I have built WooCommerce stores for food and grocery businesses in Canada, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take online orders without paying delivery app commissions?

Yes. Ordering through your own WordPress site has no per-order platform commission, only payment processing fees.

Can I update the menu myself?

Yes. With a menu built on custom fields, updating a price or adding a dish takes a minute.

Do I need a separate website for each branch?

No. One website with a page for each branch is better for SEO and easier to manage.
